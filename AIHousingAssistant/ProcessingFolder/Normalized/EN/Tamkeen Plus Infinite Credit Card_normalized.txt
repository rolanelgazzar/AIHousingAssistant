<p>FAQ</p>
<p><strong>What is Tamkeen Plus Credit Card?</strong></p>
<p>Tamkeen Plus is a high limit sharia compliant credit card allows for flexible repayment. The Card has a profit rate of 1.8% per month</p>
<p><strong>Is Tamkeen Plus credit card compatible with sharia committee?</strong></p>
<p>Tamkeen Plus credit card is based on murabaha financing where goods are purchased and sold with the customer's consent to issue the card.</p>
<p><strong>Are there any interest or profit fees on the card?</strong></p>
<p>(Tamkeen Plus credit card offers you the one of the lowest profit rates at 1.8% per month as part of your monthly allowance on your card) however, when you pay the full amount due on time, there will be no profit on the card. </p>
<p><strong>What are the payments due date on Tamkeen Plus credit card?  </strong></p>
<p>Payment is automatically made on the 25th of each calendar month. Cardholder may opt to pay (5 % of the outstanding balance or minimum of 200 <img src="media/image1.png" style="width:0.15278in;height:0.15278in" />, whichever is higher) of the balance.</p>
<p><strong>Can the customer make an early payment for Tamkeen Plus credit card?</strong></p>
<p>Yes, the customer can make a payment via Albilad Net or Albilad App</p>
<p><strong>What is the point-of-sale limit for Tamkeen Plus credit card?</strong></p>
<p>100% of the credit limit available for the card. </p>
<p><strong>What is the cash withdrawal limit for Tamkeen Plus credit card?</strong></p>
<p>Cash withdrawal limit is capped at 30% of the total card limit. </p>
<p><strong>Is it possible to add additional funds to Tamkeen plus credit card? </strong></p>
<p>Yes, a balance can be added above the credit card limit. </p>
<p><strong>What is the date of the statement of account? </strong></p>
<p>The statement of account is issued at the beginning of each calendar month</p>

<p><strong>Can I convert my welcome points into cash in my current account?</strong></p>

<p><strong>Is there a difference in the value of points when redeemed as cashback or shopping vouchers?</strong></p>
